    ABOUT THE STUDIO

    Solar PV planning, simplified

    Solara helps estimate panel count, daily energy demand, inverter and battery size, panel installation angle and indicative cost for a project in Iran. Choose a location on the map, enter your loads, and explore how the sun position and sky scenario affect estimated output.

    All results are preliminary. They are not a substitute for a site survey, weather-derived yield study, structural review or licensed electrical engineering design.
